The Rules · Crystal Clear

How we keep it clean & fair

Everyone follows the same rules. That's what makes this network work — and what protects you.

🔒

Deal details are protected

Exact address, owner, and parcel stay sealed until you place your commitment. This stops anyone from going around the network to approach the seller directly.

🤝

First committed buyer wins

Deals are assigned to the first qualified buyer who commits. No silent auctions, no games. Move when you see value.

💳

Commitment confirms you're serious

The $2,500 deal commitment filters tire-kickers and secures your position. It's non-refundable — and credited toward your assignment fee at close.

Funds are handled by a licensed title/escrow company. Clean paper, clean money, every time.

No circumvention

Using ORQ deal information to contact a seller directly, or to bypass the assignment, forfeits your network access and any commitments on file.
